Meet Our Trusted Tech Partners

PEL partners with us on technology-driven product systems and digital operations. Together we deliver reliable software integrations, data platforms, and custom IT services that keep projects on track from discovery through deployment.

Xelpr collaborates with us on product strategy, UX engineering, and end-to-end delivery. From discovery workshops to production releases, we coordinate cross-functional teams and third-party vendors to ship quality software on time.

Shopify powers scalable e-commerce platforms we build and integrate. We use their APIs and storefront tooling to deliver catalogs, payments, analytics, and custom commerce experiences for growing digital brands.

Lattice Semiconductor supports our embedded and edge-computing work. Their low-power FPGAs help us balance performance, size, and energy use in intelligent devices and industrial software systems.

Chip-Config partners with us on firmware, board-level configuration, and embedded software. Their expertise helps our systems deliver high performance, precision, and long-term reliability in production environments.

FlyTech collaborates with us on real-time data pipelines and aerial tech integrations. Together we build software for monitoring, logistics, and smart mobility that turns sensor data into actionable insights.

Tech Hive collaborates with us on IoT platforms and smart systems. We connect devices, cloud services, and user apps to automate workflows and keep data flowing across intelligent ecosystems.
