Cookie Policy

What cookies and similar technologies we use on thehashlabs.com — and how you can control them.

Privacy Policy Contact us

1. Purpose of this policy

This Cookie Policy explains how The Hash Labs uses cookies and similar technologies on our website. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy. Cookies help pages load correctly, remember preferences, and understand which content is useful — always with the goal of improving the experience for visitors and clients.

2. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your browser or device. We may also use local storage, session storage, pixels, and script-based identifiers that serve a similar purpose. Some cookies last only for a browsing session; others remain until they expire or you delete them.

3. Types of cookies we may use

  • Essential / strictly necessary: required for security, load balancing, form submission, and basic navigation. The site may not function properly without them.
  • Functional / preference: remember UI choices such as dismissed notices or preferred language where implemented.
  • Analytics / performance: help us understand aggregated traffic (pages viewed, approximate location, device type, referral sources) so we can improve content and performance.
  • Third-party / embedded: set by vendors that provide fonts, CDNs, video, maps, or analytics when their scripts are loaded on a page.

4. Why we use them

We use these technologies to:

  • Keep the website stable and secure
  • Measure which pages help visitors find services, work, or contact options
  • Diagnose performance issues and broken flows
  • Support optional features such as newsletter confirmation states

We do not use cookies to sell your personal information. Analytics, when enabled, is used in aggregate form whenever practical.

5. Third-party providers

Our pages may load resources from providers such as Google Fonts, content delivery networks, animation/3D libraries, or analytics tools. Those providers may set their own cookies or collect technical data under their policies. We select vendors based on need and configure them to avoid unnecessary tracking where possible.

If a page embeds a map, video, or social widget, interacting with that embed may trigger additional cookies from the embed provider.

6. How long cookies last

Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain for a defined period (days to months) or until you remove them. Exact durations depend on the cookie’s purpose and the vendor’s configuration. We periodically review lingering trackers and remove those we no longer need.

7. Your choices and controls

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Block all or third-party cookies
  • Delete existing cookies and site data
  • Use private / incognito browsing modes
  • Install privacy extensions that limit trackers

Blocking essential cookies may break navigation, forms, or media. Browser controls differ by product (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, etc.) — check your browser’s help center for steps.

8. Updates and contact

We may update this Cookie Policy when our tooling or legal requirements change. The latest version will always be published on this page with an updated date.
